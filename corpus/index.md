# Newspapper corpus

The project's knowledge and work, as an LLM-maintained wiki. Start here.

- The conventions for this corpus live in the repo-root [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md), under "Corpus". Read before editing here.
- [routing.md](routing.md) — which layer answers which kind of question.
- [log.md](log.md) — append-only record of every meaningful change.
- [briefs/](briefs/) — work specs: [todo](briefs/todo/) · [done](briefs/done/) · [superseded](briefs/superseded/)
- [todos/](todos/) — captured ideas, pre-spec.

**Retrieval budget:** read this page, then **at most 2–3 wiki pages**. Triage on
the summaries below — that is what they are for. Needing a fourth page is a
signal a page must split, not a licence to keep reading.

New here? [wiki/overview.md](wiki/overview.md), then
[wiki/status.md](wiki/status.md).

**Before you trust a green command in this repo**, read
[wiki/green-because-nothing-ran.md](wiki/green-because-nothing-ran.md) — eight
times a tool here reported success while reaching nothing.
